A QUICK INTRODUCTION

Know your tens.

Four players. Opposite seats are teammates. Each player gets 13 cards, ranked A, K, Q, J, 10 down to 2.

Follow the lead

Play counterclockwise and follow the opening suit if you can. If you can’t, play any card. The first off-suit play sets trump for the entire deal. Highest trump wins; otherwise highest lead-suit card wins.

Winner leads next

The opening player is selected randomly at match start. After each completed stack, the player who won it leads the next stack. Play then continues counterclockwise. The opening player is chosen randomly for every new deal.

Capture the 10s

Win with 2 tens and 7 stacks (2 points), 3 tens (3 points), or all 4 tens (5 points). Only the highest category scores.

Bank it. Or chase it.

If the other team has no tens, the winning player has 15 seconds to bank or chase all four. A chase locks the current reward. Catch all four for 5 points; if the opponent captures a ten, keep exactly your locked points. No decision defaults to banking.

Reach the target or finish the deal limit. Ties break on total tens, then total stacks. A dropped connection pauses play for up to 90 seconds. Quitting abandons the match.
