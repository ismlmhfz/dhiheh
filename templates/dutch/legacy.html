 A QUICK INTRODUCTION

Trust your memory.

  1. Start with four hidden cards. Choose your starting peeks, then remember them.
  2. On your turn, take the face-up discard or draw a hidden card. A hidden draw is visible only to you for 2 seconds. You must swap it with one of your cards.
  3. Once everyone has swapped once, a short cut window opens after each discard. Cut one or more of your cards matching its rank. A wrong cut adds one unseen penalty card. The cutter gets the next turn.
  4. Discard a Jack to peek at one of your own cards for 1 second.
  5. Call Dutch when you believe your total is lowest. Everyone else gets one final swap. No more cuts. Lowest caller scores 0; a failed caller scores twice their own total.
  6. Cut your last card and the round ends immediately: you score 0. After exactly 10 rounds, the lowest cumulative score wins.
