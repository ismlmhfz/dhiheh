 A QUICK INTRODUCTION

Trust your memory.

  1. Start with four hidden cards. Each round one player, the chooser, picks how many cards everyone may peek (0–4); pick your own and remember them.
  2. On your turn (30 seconds) take the face-up discard and swap, or draw a hidden card — seen only by you for 3 seconds — then swap it in or discard it straight away.
  3. Discard a drawn Jack to peek at one of your cards, a Queen to blind-swap one of your cards with an opponent's, or a King to lock a card face-up (or unlock one).
  4. Once everyone has played once, you may cut: throw one card matching the discard's rank until the next player plays. A wrong cut adds one unseen penalty card.
  5. Call Dutch when you believe your total is lowest. Everyone else gets one final turn. No more cuts. Strictly-lowest caller scores 0; a tie or loss scores twice their total.
  6. Cut your last card and the round ends immediately: you score 0. After exactly 10 rounds, the lowest cumulative score wins.
